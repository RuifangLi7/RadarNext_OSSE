Radar network metadata collected from NCEI at https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/access/homr/

Radar network files appended with an alphabetical letter such as "A" or "B" are individual networks.
The difference comes from which radar in "mutual pairs" (two radars whose nearest radar is each other)
was eliminated when slimming. To see how the names are set, examine thin_radar_network.py
